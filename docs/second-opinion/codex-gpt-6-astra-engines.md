Top five must-fix before the demo, in priority order:

1. **Fix false HOS approvals.** [core/hos.py:265](</Users/user/Desktop/Hackerthon/RoadStar Hackathon/services/core/hos.py:265>) uses cycle balances already clamped to zero, then requires a negative value to detect violations. Reproduced: **69 cycle hours + 2 driving hours returns feasible**; Cycle 2 driving after 72 hours without 24 hours off also passes. Calculate signed margins from unrounded totals. Check every driving interval, including its start and internal boundaries; checking mandatory rest only at the plan’s end lets later rest erase an earlier violation. Conversely, exceeding 14 on-duty/16 elapsed hours prohibits further **driving**, not continued non-driving work—don’t classify unloading alone as that violation. [Regulation, ss.13, 25–28](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-313/FullText.html).

2. **Separate daily rules from shift rules, and preserve seed provenance.** [core/hos.py:157](</Users/user/Desktop/Hackerthon/RoadStar Hackathon/services/core/hos.py:157>) implements shift limits but omits carrier-designated-day 13h/14h limits. Reproduced: 7h driving, 8h rest, 7h driving within one day passes. Add designated-day totals and daily off-duty accounting: 10h total, including 2h outside the mandatory eight, in qualifying blocks of at least 30 minutes. Rolling-24h warnings cannot establish compliance. Cycle windows also need designated-day boundaries. Correct citations: 24h-off requirement is **s.25**, Cycle 1 **s.26**, Cycle 2 **s.27**. [Regulation, ss.12–28](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-313/FullText.html).

   Ordinary shift detection, short breaks consuming the 16h window, and 36h/72h reset durations look sound; an event exactly at `as_of` incorrectly reports the preceding status. Seeding is acceptable **as synthetic scenario history**, not verified availability: [api/main.py:359](</Users/user/Desktop/Hackerthon/RoadStar Hackathon/services/api/main.py:359>) relabels it `duty_log`. Propagate synthetic provenance into HOS/rescue/snapshot results. Cycle 2 seeding also introduces an extra prior driving hour; requested 60h remaining becomes 59h. Never silently normalize an unknown/US cycle to Canadian Cycle 1.

3. **Finish the simulated trip and synchronize each tick.** [sim/main.py:218](</Users/user/Desktop/Hackerthon/RoadStar Hackathon/services/sim/main.py:218>) marks final-stop trucks done and returns no ping. The hero never exits, finalizes detention, or transmits its off-duty transition. Add departure through the gate, enough outside pings for debounce, then a parked heartbeat and duty event. At [sim/main.py:287](</Users/user/Desktop/Hackerthon/RoadStar Hackathon/services/sim/main.py:287>), telemetry triggers exceptions **before** the API clock advances. Silent straight-line fallback, endpoint teleportation, and rescue assignments never changing truck movement are obvious stage challenges.
