Questions to expect

Four you will be asked, and the honest answer to each

“Why not just use the TMS?”It can compute this — after someone types the arrival and departure times in. Nobody types them in.
“Does the dwell prediction generalise?”We don’t know. Some cities have a sample of nine. That is why the sample size is on the screen.
“Is that money you can actually collect?”No. It is exposure. Contract eligibility, real rates and payment records are not in this file.
“What would you build next?”Backhaul. Thirty-five percent of legs run empty, but only twelve percent of the distance — they are short city moves.
